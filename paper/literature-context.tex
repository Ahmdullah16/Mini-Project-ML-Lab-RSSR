Residual prediction has an established precedent in VDSR \cite{Kim_2016_CVPR}; our compact residual branch is not a reproduction of that network. Learned enlargement can use sub-pixel rearrangement \cite{Shi_2016_CVPR}, while iterative back-projection offers a different reconstruction design \cite{Haris_2018_CVPR}. Our tail uses PixelShuffle and does not implement a back-projection network.

Channel recalibration is studied by squeeze-and-excitation networks \cite{Hu_2018_CVPR}; second-order attention models richer feature statistics \cite{Dai_2019_CVPR}. Our average-pooling gate does not implement second-order covariance attention. Non-local networks provide a related context-aggregation mechanism \cite{Wang_2018_CVPR}, while Swin Transformer introduces a hierarchical shifted-window design \cite{Liu_2021_ICCV}. Our fixed-window block lacks that full shifted-window hierarchy.

SRGAN illustrates an adversarial route to perceptual reconstruction \cite{Ledig_2017_CVPR}; learned perceptual similarity provides another evaluation perspective \cite{Zhang_2018_CVPR}. Neither adversarial training nor LPIPS measurement is established by our recorded experiments. These references explain why visual plausibility, pixel error and physical fidelity are distinct assessment questions.

The foundational LR-to-HR convolutional mapping is described by SRCNN \cite{dong2014srcnn}; EDSR strengthens residual CNN reconstruction \cite{lim2017edsr}. RCAN adds residual channel recalibration \cite{zhang2018rcan}, while SwinIR applies residual Swin blocks to restoration \cite{liang2021swinir}. These works establish architectural precedents, not comparable results under our data and compute budget.

HAT combines channel and window attention with overlapping cross-attention \cite{chen2023hat}; a CNN/attention/Transformer combination alone therefore does not establish novelty. MambaIRv2 provides an attentive state-space restoration alternative \cite{guo2025mambairv2}. Neither HAT nor MambaIRv2 was trained or evaluated in the recorded local study.

DiffFuSR combines diffusion-based RGB restoration and multispectral fusion \cite{sarmad2025difffusr}. GeoSR-Bench motivates evaluating downstream Earth-monitoring utility beyond image fidelity \cite{li2026geosr}. The latter is cited as a preprint; this study does not claim to reproduce either method or its published results. WorldStrat and SEN2VENUS provide candidate real paired data \cite{cornebise2022worldstrat,michel2022sen2venus}. The published SEN2VENUS correction concerns the dataset URL \cite{michel2023correction}; it is not a measured registration improvement. Eligibility still requires file-version, bands, masks, calibration and alignment checks.

Scaled dot-product and multi-head attention follow established Transformer foundations \cite{vaswani2017attention}. Our fixed-window, pre-normalized block has a different task and topology from the original translation model. Adam supplies adaptive moment estimates for stochastic optimization \cite{kingma2015adam}; the citation uses its 2015 conference year, while the preprint began in 2014. SSIM measures reference-image structural similarity \cite{wang2004ssim}. Our recorded uniform $7\times7$ window and population-moment convention differs from the original Gaussian-weighted implementation; those settings must accompany every comparison.

It is not a complete extraction or reproduction of each paper's datasets, losses, hardware and evaluation protocol. External performance claims are not transferred into the local result tables.
